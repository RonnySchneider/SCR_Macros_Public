SCR_CopyXSectionsToPlanview

  • had a request where they wanted the automatically created XSections or Profile in normal Planview as DXF, so that the engineer could scribble around in them in AutoCAD
  • first define the origin in Planview
  • then change to Sheetview and define the two corners, a fake (clockwise) mouse gesture will select on each sheet what is inside that rectangle and re-create it in Planview
  • either select single sheets or a Sheetset in the Project Explorer
  • the macro will ignore, during runtime, everything that’s not a “Profile sheet set” or “Cross section sheet set” since the other ones usually only contain Dynaviews
  • the macro will retrieve the horizontal scale from the Sheetset and scale it accordingly, double check is never a bad idea
