SCR_RelocateSheetset

  • with this macro you can move a Sheetset to another Planset
  • i.e. if you want to clean up a bit at a later point and organize the Sheetsets by categories
  • will also make it simpler to select them for plotting
  • a Planset is basically just a category/folder with a name; it does not hold any dimension or scale information
  • like Sheets and Sheetsets it can hold drawing entities like a frame/text/logo and inherits those down to them; I usually have the main frame/stamp/logo on Planset-Level
  • so, moving a Sheetset to another Planset should only be done if the page layout (frame location/size) is the same; it doesn't make much sense to move a Sheetset with A3 layout to a A0 Planset; but it is possible and may require some scaling afterwards

  • TBC only allows to move a Sheet to another Sheetset within the Properties Pane, but for some inexplicable reason you can't relocate a Sheetset, at least until now
