SCR_LockViews

  • move/zoom in up to 3 Planviews at the same time
  • the views can have different viewfilters enabled, making it easier to show/inspect different features/layers/overlays at the same location at the same time
  • the rotation of each view is irrelevant
  • the tickbox "View Lock enabled" activates/deactivates the macros functionality
  • without "Scale Lock" enabled the cross hair is shown in all views
    • the cross-hair outline is always the one from the view that covers the smallest area
    • if you move one of the selected plan views the other selected ones are automatically centered on the "senders" location
    • each view keeps its rotation and scale
  • with "Scale Lock" the center and zoom for all views is the same, no cross-hair needed
