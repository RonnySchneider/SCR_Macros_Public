SCR_AreaReport

  • request from the Civils crew, they have to report the area in sqm of the pickup, material being placed per day, and the location along the reference alignment
  • simple Macro to report the area of a Polygon (will be closed automatically) and the 2 Chainages where it crosses an Alignment into a CSVFile
  • added option to manually pick the chainages, in case the pickup area is just a single lane and doesn’t cross the alignment
  • afterwards just copy and paste the values into the Excel Template File (is included in the macro folder)
