SCR_MoveAlongVector

  • the vector can be defined by 2 points
  • or as the normal vector of a 3point plane, if the plane is defined clockwise the vector faces “upwards” (counterclockwise it faces “downwards”)
  • a positive offset follows the direction of the vector (negative is the opposite direction)
  • is handy to move bearing pad outlines perpendicular to the shelf or to create leaning bolts
  • can also be used to move those outlines until one of the stringlines nodes touches a surface, i.e. girder underside (works only forward along the vector, define it accordingly) – the touch mode works currently only with stringlines
