SCR_ViewFilter

  • for the time being I left the button to update the state of the checkboxes; but it shouldn't be necessary anymore to click it
  • it's intended to make finding/enabling/disabling Layers and Surfaces in large projects more efficient
  • since Layers and Surfaces are in the same list it can become a real ordeal to find them, scrolling up and down ......
  • this macro gives you an additional 2 lists for layers and 2 for surfaces
  • the pre-filter is case insensitive and only needs parts separated by a space
  • if you load another project the macro will close itself
  • I’ve added it to my Quick-Access-Toolbar, together with plan view rotation
