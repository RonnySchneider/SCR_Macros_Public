SCR_AutoCorner

  • if you have 2 lines with a gap, i.e. around a corner, it will compute the intersection point (IP) between the two closest line segments and extend Line 1 to that IP and further to Line 2 – it will close the smaller gap between both lines ends – if they touch each other already, the remaining gap is closed
  • you don’t have to worry about the line directions, the macro will reverse them if necessary
  • added an option to remove 2DZeroLengthElements, if the lines would start/end with a ZeroLength or exact vertical 3DSegment the builtin intersection function would fail – considering the intended purpose of this macro it is unlikely that lines will have exact vertical segments anyway


  • there are two modes for the elevation computation of the IP
