SCR_SweepBetweenLines

  • use 2 given lines and compute cross connections at a chosen interval
  • those lines can be extended a certain distance or the macro can try to intersect them with a DTM
  • added Bench-Pattern option, Lines will be drawn from high to low



  • can be handy to compute additional/smooth line work around a corner, as in the example above the given triangulation is too coarse or actually wrong
