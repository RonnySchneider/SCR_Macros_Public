SCR_ABReportWithBearing

  • point to point comparison
  • you need to drag and drop the “AB Arrow Block.vcl” from the macro folders into the project, that contains the arrow block
  • design data must exist as points (simple CAD or Point with name) in a separate layer
  • dx/dy is computed square to a chosen bearing



  • the whole text can be colored in just one color
  • or, after exploding the multiline texts you can use SCR_ThreshColorText to color the individual lines
  • in case you’re missing the text Whiteout option after exploding them you might have to convert it back to “Multiline”Text, even if it is just 1 line, press F12 and run the built-in “ConvertToMultilineText”
