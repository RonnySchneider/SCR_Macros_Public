SCR_EditSideslope

  • for my current feasibility studies I preferred to use linestrings with sideslopes attached to them, that way I was able to simply use the smooth/curved horizontal segment option and just drag the lines around, and the sideslope/DTM changing immediately
  • but I was annoyed by the fact that you couldn’t just simply change the start position of the sideslope, only the end, typical TBC shortcoming
  • so, I created a macro that highlights all sideslopes on the screen, makes it easier to find them, especially in 3D view
  • once a Sideslope is selected it will highlight the line, and also the section on the line it covers
  • and it lets you change some settings which you can’t get to anymore
