SCR_GirderOutline

  • quickly create girder outlines around center lines, it only uses the start and end node of each stringline
  • the outline will be centered over the selected line, hence the line can be shorter or longer than the girder length, but must define a centered position for the outline; usually it will be the shorter stringline between the hold down bolt locations


  • in this example the left side is shifted forward (half the entered skew distance, and the right side that same amount backwards)
